IDEAS

- STAGGER RATE -
A percentage of how well you are doing correct staggering s over t time in combat over previous c combat encounters. So in combat 1 you did 70% uptime of correct stagger, in combat 2 40%, in combat 3 50% = 160%/3 = 53% stagger over those previous fights. 

Then you go into combat now and you can see your stagger rate fluctuate live while in combat and you can see delta vs. historic stagger. 

Example:
Historic stagger = 53%
Current stagger = 60%
Stagger Delta = +7% 

The visual should have the current stagger, the historic stagger for the last n fights and the current stagger pct so the user can see how they are doing. 
---


- FLURRY UPTIME -
A percentage that shows how much flurry uptime you are able to keep in your fights. This is interesting because it is not random, and its driven by correct and high stagger rate. And it is an indicator that will increase your damage if managed well. 

Example:
Historic flurry = 43%
Current flurry = 60%
Flurry Delta = +17% 
---


- WINDFURY PROC RATE - 
A high percentage of windfury procs can also be controlled because you can time when to use stormstrike and how you move in combat. Historic is WF procs in last n fights / fights in the last t minutes. 

Example:
Historic windfury = 23%
Current windfury = 60%
windfury Delta = +37% 
---


- DPS -
DPS meters do one thing and that is to compare you to other people, but it does not compare you to yourself, which is the most important part. In order to be better you must see how you are doing by yourself. 
By having a simple DPS number with comparisons to running avg previous last fights you can more easily see how you are doing in a meaningfull way.  

Example:
Historic avg. DPS = 166
Previous fight = 250
Current DPS = 300
DPS Delta = +134 (+44%) = (1 - (historic / current)) = n
